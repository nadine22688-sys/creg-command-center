Investment Options for Filipino Employees
General education guide only — not financial advice. All investments carry risk. Do your own research.
Invest in This Order
Build your foundation before chasing returns
1
Emergency Fund first (3–6 months expenses)
Non-negotiable safety net before any investment.
2
Max out MP2 — tax-free 6–9% returns
Best risk-adjusted return available to Philippine employees.
3
Pay off high-interest debt (credit cards first)
3% monthly interest = 36% guaranteed annual return when eliminated.
4
Invest for long-term wealth (UITF, stocks, REIT)
Only invest money you won't need for 5+ years. Start small, stay consistent.
Investment Options at a Glance
PAG-IBIG MP2
Low Risk
Min. Investment₱500/month
Target Return6–9% p.a.
Lock-in5 years (re-enrollable)
Tax on GainsNone ✅
Safest & best return for employees. Government-backed, tax-free. Use the MP2 tab to project your gains.
Cooperatives
Low–Med Risk
Min. InvestmentVaries (₱1,000+)
Target Return5–12% p.a.
Lock-inMembership-based
Tax on GainsNone (registered)
Check if MMC has an employee cooperative. Community-based, regular dividends, often better rates than banks.
UITF (Unit Investment Trust Fund)
Low–High Risk
Min. Investment₱1,000–₱10,000
Target Return3–15%+ (by type)
Lock-inNone (liquid)
Tax on GainsNone (TRAIN Law)
Types: Money Market (~3%), Bond (~5%), Balanced (~7%), Equity (10–15%+). Available at BDO, BPI, Metrobank. Great for beginners — diversified, professionally managed.
PSE Stocks / FMETF
High Risk
Min. Investment₱1,000 (COL/GInvest)
Target ReturnAvg 10–12% long-term
Lock-inNone (sell anytime)
Tax0.6% stock transaction
Beginners: start with FMETF (index fund tracking the whole PSE). Apps: COL Financial, GInvest. Best for 10+ year horizons only.
High-Yield Savings (HYSA)
Very Low Risk
Min. Investment₱0 (Tonik, Maya)
Target Return2–7% p.a.
Lock-inNone (liquid)
Tax on Gains20% final tax
Tonik (up to 6%), Maya (up to 15% promo), ING, UnionBank. Ideal for emergency fund and short-term goals. Far better than traditional savings accounts.
REITs (Real Estate)
Medium Risk
Min. Investment₱1,000 via PSE
Target Return5–8% dividend yield
Lock-inNone (listed stocks)
Tax on Dividends10%
Own a share of malls & offices for ₱1,000. Examples: AREIT, MREIT, DDMPR, FILRT. Regular quarterly dividends. Great real-estate exposure without buying property.
Simple Beginner Portfolio: 40% MP2 + 30% Equity UITF + 20% Cooperative + 10% HYSA (liquid). Automate contributions every payday. Pay yourself first — before any spending.
